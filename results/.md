### Flood-Patch Analysis

The detected flood mask was converted from raster to vector and subsequently analyzed as individual single-part flood geometries.

The polygon-based analysis produced a total geometry area of **24.4016 km²**, compared with **24.3894 km²** from the raster-based calculation.

| Method                       | Detected Area |
| ---------------------------- | ------------: |
| Raster pixel calculation     |   24.3894 km² |
| Polygon geometry calculation |   24.4016 km² |
| Difference                   |   ~0.0122 km² |
| Relative difference          |        ~0.05% |

The close agreement between the raster and polygon calculations provides a consistency check between the raster-derived and vector-derived area measurements.
